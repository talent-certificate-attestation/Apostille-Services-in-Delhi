## Apostille or normal attestation: what is the difference?

The Ministry of External Affairs (MEA) provides two forms of authentication for Indian documents intended for use abroad:

- **Apostille:** For eligible documents intended for countries that accept apostilles under the Hague Apostille Convention.
- **Normal attestation:** For destinations where apostille is not accepted and further legalisation is required.

The MEA confirms that apostille is used for eligible personal and educational documents, while normal attestation applies to countries where apostille is not accepted. Always verify the destination’s current requirements before applying.

## How the apostille process works in Delhi

The process depends on the document type and whether it is eligible for e-Sanad. For documents processed through the standard route, the MEA describes these general stages:

1. **Pre-authentication:** The document is authenticated by the designated authority in the state or union territory where it was issued. Educational and personal documents may have different preliminary authorities.
2. **Submission:** The document is submitted through an authorised service provider for MEA apostille or attestation.
3. **Return:** The processed document is collected or returned through the applicable service.

The MEA states that documents are not accepted directly from individuals at its CPV Division counter in New Delhi; applicants must use the authorised submission channels. 

For Delhi-issued documents, confirm the applicable preliminary authentication authority based on the document category before submitting originals.

## What to check before submitting your documents

A document review can help identify common issues before processing:

- **Names:** Check consistency with the passport and supporting records.
- **Completeness:** Confirm all pages, annexures and required certificates are included.
- **Issuing authority:** Identify the authority responsible for preliminary authentication.
- **Format:** Check whether the process requires an original or an eligible true copy.
- **Destination requirements:** Confirm whether apostille is accepted and whether the receiving organisation has additional instructions.

MEA apostille authenticates the relevant official signature or seal; it does not certify the contents of the document.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is apostille accepted in every country?

No. Apostille applies to eligible documents intended for countries that accept it under the Hague Apostille Convention. Other destinations may require normal attestation or legalisation.

### Can any document be apostilled?

No. Documents must meet the applicable authentication requirements and be eligible for the process. Requirements vary by document category and issuing authority.

### Does apostille verify the contents of my certificate?

No. MEA apostille authenticates the relevant official signature or seal, not the substantive contents of the document.

### Do I submit documents directly to the MEA in Delhi?

The MEA states that individuals should submit documents through authorised service providers rather than directly at its CPV Division counter.

### How can I confirm which process I need?

Check the destination country’s requirements and the official MEA apostille and attestation guidance before submitting your documents.
